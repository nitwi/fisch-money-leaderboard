## Collection limits

Collection stopped after a browser access restriction prevented further Roblox profile inspection. A retry of an HTTPS profile was rejected by automatic approval review at the Roblox origin; no alternate browser route or API was used after that rejection. This is not evidence that a particular Roblox account has blocked access, is banned, or has no friends. Sixty planned source accounts have no saved website inspection in this checkpoint.

The lists for n6sy, DerpyPoopManXD and JPaltaa reached their accessible end but contained unavailable identity cards. ismyaimbotworking reached the final page but exact unique IDs did not equal the displayed total. Those are partial records. zerealali contains only the first observed page.

The retained historical record for 1234mysterio contains 18 exact IDs from preserved observations on pages 1 and 2. A browser-runtime interruption occurred before the larger in-memory historical arrays were exported. Only exact rows and actual times recoverable from prior observations were saved; unexported rows were not reconstructed or counted. Other historical inspections of 75percent3r, anthonqt13 and BigBoyyChriss have no retained friend journal record and do not count toward the saved source coverage. Navigation to aurunqwer failed, and casketdream was interrupted before verification. Navigation to ody_odinho failed before a readable list was obtained.

Unavailable cards using `/users/-1/profile` are not valid account nodes. Displayed counts, unique saved IDs and page counts remain separate. A partial observation can establish a recorded positive connection, but absent identities remain unknown. Per-item times retain the actual page observation time; snapshot build time is not a claim that every relationship was checked then.
