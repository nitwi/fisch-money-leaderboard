# Fisch Money Leaderboard

Independent, public, screenshot-backed history of the Fisch Top C$ board. The charts show *observed net held-balance changes*, not verified sales, earnings, or misconduct.

Source window: October 10–November 25, 2026 leaderboard days (latest photo captured November 26 at approximately 00:10 Central) (America/Chicago); later captures may be appended. Each original screenshot remains linked to its captured rank slots.

**Current coverage:** 53 original board captures and 235 board PNGs form 51 reconciled screenshot sessions and **46 displayed daily points across 46 leaderboard days**, all complete Top 50. One additional original player-profile screenshot updates a single photographed balance without asserting a new rank. I read and checked **2,510 of 2,513 pictured rank slots** directly from the board originals, including all **49 complete Top-50 captures**. Three partly pictured historical raw readings remain cropped/unreadable; 137 raw rank slots were not photographed, but every displayed point has all 50 confirmed ranks. **October 18 has no photo or daily point.** The latest complete Saturday photo is the end-of-week settlement baseline; Sunday starts a separate ordinary point. Every public measurement is photo-checked; no OCR-only suggestions are published.

## Refresh the data

Source observations are **only original screenshots**. The bundled `data/source/data/checkpoints.csv` and `data/source/data/evidence_manifest.csv` supply capture timing and original PNG indexes, not names or balances. The previous Excel-derived `observations.csv` is intentionally not an input and is not stored here. Preserve screenshot bytes and stable IDs. `data/screenshot_extractions.csv` is only an internal reading aid, never a public chart-data input; `data/reviewed.csv` and `data/photo_verified.csv` contain readings checked directly against the original images. The `data/photo_audits.csv` and `data/photo_corrections.csv` ledgers record exactly which original image/rank ranges were inspected and how OCR ambiguities were resolved.

For future captures, use `python tools/import_capture.py --date YYYY-MM-DD --time HH:MM --image '1-16=/path/top.png' ...` to preview the original PNG hashes, rank spans and Chicago/UTC capture time; repeat with `--apply` only after verifying those details. It copies the unmodified images under `site/evidence/screenshots/<checkpoint_id>/` and appends the two source indexes. `python tools/ocr_draft.py` (RapidOCR), `python tools/ocr_tesseract.py`, and `python tools/extract_screenshots.py` may help organize candidates, **but they never publish numbers**. Read each original screenshot, record the image/rank spans personally checked in `data/photo_audits.csv` and direct transcriptions/corrections in `data/photo_corrections.csv`, then run `python tools/promote_photo_review.py`. Resolve exact Roblox identities with `python tools/resolve_roblox.py`; `python tools/build.py` validates image hashes and emits only photo-confirmed observations to `site/data.json`. Run `python tools/report_missing.py` to distinguish unphotographed days/rank bands from unreadable photographed rows. Run `python -m unittest discover -s tests` and `python tools/smoke_site.py` before publication.

For a later **single-player profile photo**, use `python tools/import_profile_update.py --image 'C:/path/Screenshot 2026-11-12 233850.png' --day 2026-11-12 --username ismyaimbotworking --roblox-id 1316700350 --balance 269064293664 --source-checkpoint 2026-11-12_2258 --source-rank 2 --source-evidence EV0169` to preview exact screenshot filename times, matched Roblox identity, prior board amount and SHA-256; repeat with `--apply` after personally checking the original. Its independent `data/profile_updates.csv` and unchanged `site/evidence/profiles/` image are validated on build. JSON schema version 6 preserves the board row's original `board_balance`, `board_at`, and rank proof, while `balance_evidence` and `at` identify the later personal balance photo. It changes only that player's balance for the day; it does not invent or replace a full-board photo, a new rank, or any other player's value.

The site is deployed to GitHub Pages from `site/` via `.github/workflows/pages.yml`. No ShockieWockie dashboard or Worker resources are used. The public URL is `https://nitwi.github.io/fisch-money-leaderboard/`.

## Account Networker

`site/networker.html` compares exact Roblox accounts from the ledger and any explicitly resolved reference accounts in `site/network-data.json`. Dashboard and Standout Analysis link a selected account directly to the viewer. The first snapshot includes all 69 ledger accounts and the exact reference account `Lunary810` (12013950464); the reference label carries no automated allegation.

The viewer separates direct social observations, shared friends, explained username patterns, and equipped-avatar asset overlap. Username comparisons look for distinctive shared stems, numbered variants, separators, token patterns and common character substitutions. Generic words, short fragments, digit-only overlap and weak random-string resemblance are suppressed. Each positive result explains its rule; the label is a review hint, not an ownership probability. These deterministic comparisons run locally with no live AI call, and they never merge identities or create graph edges. The older edit-distance helper remains only for compatibility; it no longer ranks or labels the review queue. Avatar overlap is shared exact asset IDs divided by the union of equipped IDs, with clothing/accessories shown separately from body and animation assets. Matching equipped item sets are not sufficient to establish an identical rendered avatar or common ownership. Side-by-side thumbnails and item/profile links let a reviewer inspect the actual comparison. Avatars are current at their own scan timestamps, not at the historical board photo time.

The social graph draws only friendship or directed following edges supported by recorded observations. Direct friend lists inspected through normal Roblox website pages are attributed separately from API results. Unavailable and partial lists remain explicit; a failed request or a list that has not been inspected is never a zero connection count. Initial unauthenticated probes returned HTTP 403 for friends and 401 for followers/followings, and those original failed API records remain preserved. Roblox [removed unauthenticated followers/followings access effective May 4, 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-data-stewardship-principles-cookie-based-api-update/4579220/); the [current friends reference](https://create.roblox.com/docs/cloud/reference/domains/friends) labels the list endpoints Cookie-only. A generic friends 403 does not diagnose an account's privacy, age, region, or ban status.

The bounded public API collector is `python tools/scan_networker.py --target Lunary810 --output /path/to/api-snapshot.json`; repeat `--target USERNAME` for additional exact reference accounts. No Roblox credentials or third-party proxy are used by that collector. It paces requests, respects rate-limit waits, preserves endpoint/error/coverage metadata, and writes atomically. The manual **Collect account comparison snapshot** workflow produces a downloadable artifact for review; it does not change the published site. Reapply the website observation journal with the offline builder below before publishing a fresh API snapshot. Browser reloads load the saved snapshot rather than rescan Roblox.

## Data interpretation

- Both site pages display actual screenshot, profile-proof, patch-event and standout photo timestamps in the viewer's browser timezone with the local time-zone abbreviation; the displayed IANA timezone is shown above the board/analysis. The *leaderboard-day labels*, chart-day slots, Saturday settlement assignment, and shareable `day`/`from`/`to` dates remain America/Chicago leaderboard cycles, so visitors do not see different daily histories. When a local photo date differs from its leaderboard-day label (for example, an early-AM rollover or a Chicago late-night photo viewed in Eastern time), the player history prints the full local photo date and time. Elapsed-time calculations continue to use the original UTC instants, independent of the viewer's timezone.
- All C$ amounts are exact decimal integers. A blank is unknown, never zero.
- Outside Saturday settlements, no-multiplier net rate = observed balance difference / elapsed UTC hours. For a multi-photo window crossing a Saturday settlement, the **weekly-normalized net change** = the same exact endpoint difference ÷ (actual elapsed UTC hours / 168), with a `C$/week` label and the explicit observed photo span; a short range extrapolates to a seven-day equivalent, not actual weekly earnings. Approximate timestamps and skipped checkpoints are labeled; a single settlement jump has no hourly rate.
- Rainbow 1.5× base-equivalent = a positive observed net delta ÷ 1.5, displayed as a hypothetical **window total** and, for multi-photo ranges, a `C$/week` normalized base-equivalent when Saturday settlement is included or `/hr` otherwise. The unadjusted no-multiplier average is always labeled separately. This is a *counterfactual*, not proof of sales or gross earnings; declines and spending are not divided by 1.5.
- Neighbor gaps refer to the actual ranked player ahead/behind at each checkpoint. Each change compares the **same two Roblox accounts head-to-head across both photo-confirmed checkpoints**, even when they swap ranks; it never subtracts the player's old gap to a different rank neighbor. The direction and color are from the **row player's perspective**: closing the gap to the player above is positive, but losing the same amount of lead over the player below is negative. Growing a lead below is positive, while falling further behind the player above is negative. When positions reverse, the label says who overtook or lost the lead and gives the net head-to-head swing. The previous rank neighbor is named separately when it changed; if the current opponent has no earlier confirmed balance, the comparison says the prior head-to-head is unknown instead of implying progress. Unchanged head-to-head gaps are neutral. On Nov 21, Apathy overtook LowTierNova: from 16,398,191,512 C$ behind on Friday to 25,658,389,587 C$ ahead on Saturday (a 42,056,581,099 C$ swing), positive for Apathy and negative for LowTierNova.
- A departure can only be asserted across adjacent fully pictured and photo-checked Top-50 captures. Otherwise the player is merely not observed. If an image crops the amount, that rank stays unknown rather than borrowing a value from another day.
- The default 8B C$ highlight is a net balance threshold, not an allegation.
